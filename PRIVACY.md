# Privacy

This page summarizes the privacy model of Secure Password Vault by PracticalForge Software.

For the full Privacy Policy, visit:

https://practicalforge.com/privacy.html

## Offline-first vault data

Secure Password Vault is designed as an offline-first Windows desktop app.

Your saved vault data stays on your own Windows computer.

PracticalForge Software does not collect, read, upload, sell, or share your:

- Saved passwords
- Master password
- Vault password
- Secure notes
- Bank details
- Personal records
- Private lists
- 2FA backup codes
- Vault database files
- Vault backup files
- Private vault contents

## License activation

Paid editions may use internet access for license activation and verification.

Your vault contents are not uploaded during license activation or license verification.

## Support email

If you contact support, your email address, message, and any details you choose to provide may be used to respond to your request.

Do not send private vault data to support.

## Third-party services

PracticalForge Software may use third-party services for:

- Website hosting
- DNS and website security
- Email routing
- Product delivery
- Payment processing
- License-related purchase workflows

Examples include Cloudflare, Gumroad, and payment providers.

These services may process technical, purchase, or payment-related information according to their own policies.

## Master password

PracticalForge Software does not store your master password.

If you lose your master password, PracticalForge Software cannot recover, reset, bypass, or decrypt your vault.

## User responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- Keeping their master password safe
- Keeping backups secure
- Protecting their own Windows computer
- Deleting unneeded exported browser CSV files after import
- Avoiding sending private vault data through email or support messages
