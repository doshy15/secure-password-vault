## Master password responsibility

Secure Password Vault is offline-first.

PracticalForge Software does not store your master password, vault password, vault contents, backup files, or private records on a server.

Because of this, PracticalForge Software cannot reset, recover, bypass, or decrypt a lost master password.

Users are responsible for keeping their master password safe and maintaining secure backups.
